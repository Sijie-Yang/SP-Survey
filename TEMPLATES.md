# Template library

**232 perception studies from published papers, rebuilt as ready-to-run surveys**, plus 10 demos and examples. Each template has the questions, the task design and a media library, so you can rerun the study, swap in your own images, or use it as a starting point.

Browse, preview, use or download every template (ZIP with the survey and its media) at **[sp-survey.org/templates](https://sp-survey.org/templates)**. This list is a snapshot from 2026-11-27; the website is always current.

> A template is our rebuild of a study from its paper. Some use the paper's own stimuli, others follow the method with new images; each template page says which. The papers belong to their authors, and none of them endorse this project. If a template of your paper needs a correction, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Sijie-Yang/SP-Survey/issues).

## At a glance

- **Stimuli:** Scene photos 101 · Street view 81 · Rendered 29 · Indoor 14 · Panorama 12 · Video 8 · Audio 8 · In situ 3 · Map 2 · Window view 1
- **Tasks:** Multi-scale rating 105 · Rating 70 · Questionnaire 69 · Pairwise 40 · Custom page 33 · Choose / rank 23 · Open answers 15 · Map annotation 1
- **Study places:** 41 countries and regions. Most common: Mainland China 84 · United States 33 · United Kingdom 20 · Germany 11 · Singapore 8 · Italy 8 · Spain 7 · South Korea 7
- **Years:** 1876–2027
